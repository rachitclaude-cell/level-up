Questions

Is it safe for teens?

Every plan starts with a safety check and a level test. Moves that load sore areas are left out, and the plan moves you up at most one level per block. If something hurts, stop and get it checked.

Do I need equipment?

No. Every plan works with your body, a wall, a towel and a sturdy table. Dumbbells, a backpack, a pull-up bar or bands unlock more moves.

Where does my data go?

Your plan, ticks and videos stay on your device, and videos are never uploaded. The AI coach only sees what you choose to send it.

When do paid plans start?

Soon. Until then everything is free. When they open, a parent or guardian pays for anyone under 18 and can cancel any time.

Level-Up gives general exercise guidance for healthy people aged 13 and up. It is not medical advice. If something hurts, stop and get it checked.
